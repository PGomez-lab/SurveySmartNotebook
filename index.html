Foresight (FS) - sighting this point from the current setup

Backsight (BS) - starting a new setup here

Standard procedure: the instrument turns an angle BETWEEN two lines - the Backsight (to the previous point) and the Foresight (to the next one). Each line is its own sighting - its own Tripod and its own raw angle reading - since a Backsight can come from a different setup (e.g. a free-station resection) than the Foresight. Record each line's own reading first, then work out the Interior Angle from the two.
